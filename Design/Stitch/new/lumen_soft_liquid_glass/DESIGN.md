## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an executive-function companion app supporting neurodivergent adults managing ADHD. The visual direction balances tranquility, spatial breathing room, and low-stimulation focus with tangible feedback. 

To prevent cognitive overload, sensory overwhelm, and executive paralysis:
- **Style Archetype:** Soft Liquid Glass combined with tactile, airy surface physics. Glass elements use subdued opacity, delicate borders, and minimal refraction—avoiding chaotic highlights, aggressive blurs, or high-energy neon shifts.
- **Atmospheric Palette:** Warm, stabilizing surfaces anchor the viewport, using matte cream-lilac and soothing teal rather than overstimulating corporate blues or hyper-vibrant purples.
- **Copy & Tone (pt-BR):** Gentle, compassionate, and actionable. Microcopy avoids shame-inducing idioms, patronizing praise, or alarming deadlines. Commands are atomic, bite-sized, and reassuring (e.g., *"Um passo de cada vez"*, *"Respire fundo"*, *"Vamos começar pelo menor?"*).

## Colors

The palette establishes clear visual anchors that guide focus without inducing anxiety.

### Core Roles
- **Primary / Action (`#1FAF8A`):** Anchors forward progress, task verification, and primary actions. Supported by `#3DDC97` (active/bright focus) and `#E0F7EF` (calming container wash).
- **Secondary / Focus Rhythm (`#8B7BC8`):** Restful lavender designated for passive time-blocking, reflection, and cognitive pauses. Accompanied by `#F0EDF9` for secondary badge surfaces.
- **Tertiary / Descongelar (Anti-Paralysis) (`#FF8A5C`):** A warm, compassionate peach-coral dedicated to gentle interventions when the user feels stuck, stalled, or overwhelmed. Paired with `#FFF0EA` as a low-stimulation alert wash.
- **Neutral / Foundations:**
  - **Light Canvas:** Base surface is `#F6F1FF` (solid cream-lilac), cards rest on `#FFFFFF` bordered with `#E8E4DF`.
  - **Dark Canvas:** Base surface is `#16161A`, cards shift to `#212127` with delicate `#2F2F37` borders.
  - **Text Layers:** Deep charcoal `#1A1A1E` for primary readability (avoiding pure `#000000` glare) and `#6B6560` for stabilizing contextual guidance.

## Typography

Typographic scale is structured around **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its generous x-height, wide open counters, and reassuring geometric terminals. It maximizes legibility during periods of cognitive fatigue and visual drifting.

### Hierarchy & Legibility Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Used strictly for single-sentence anchoring phrases (*"Como podemos começar hoje?"*). Never stack more than two lines of display text.
- **Body:** Body text uses comfortable line heights (150% minimum) to prevent tracking loss while reading multi-step sequences.
- **Labels & Micro-prompts:** High-legibility, slightly tracked metadata to help users scan priority, time estimates, and state tags without friction.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an uncluttered, single-focus container philosophy designed to reduce decision paralysis.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Mobile (Base, 360px - 599px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins. Dynamic vertical spacing prioritizes the "One Thing at a Time" principle, keeping competing elements below the fold or tucked away.
- **Tablet (600px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with centered content max-width (`680px` for task flows) to avoid expansive eye travel.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with a pinned, calm breathing frame (`max-width: 960px`). Content containers do not stretch infinitely; empty space remains a neutral canvas to preserve cognitive clarity.

### Rhythm Principles
- Component groupings utilize `space-sm` (8px) for tightly bound metadata, `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, and `space-xl` (40px) to separate distinct steps or routine phases.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system communicates visual hierarchy through calm tactile layering and translucent surface treatment, avoiding aggressive drop shadows.

### Soft Liquid Glass Layers
- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat, non-reflective `#F6F1FF` (dark: `#16161A`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Solid White Cards):** `#FFFFFF` (dark: `#212127`) with a 1px solid border `#E8E4DF` (dark: `#2F2F37`). Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 26, 30, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Liquid Glass Floating Elements):** Backdrop blur of `16px` to `24px` coupled with a semi-transparent surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` in light mode, `rgba(33, 33, 39, 0.75)` in dark mode). Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)` with an inner soft specular glow `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Focus / Unstuck Modal Overlays):** Ambient, ultra-diffused tint shadow `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(31, 175, 138, 0.12)` (or tertiary tint `rgba(255, 138, 92, 0.14)` for anti-paralysis modals).

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic, tactile roundness to soften interaction points and remove sharp visual tensions that can amplify perceived task difficulty.

### Radius Assignments
- **Small Controls (Chips, Badges, Mini-buttons):** `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`).
- **Standard Inputs, Action Buttons, Task Rows:** `rounded-xl` (24px / `1.5rem`).
- **Containers, Routine Cards, Modal Sheets:** `rounded-3xl` (28px - 32px) for a soft, pebble-like aesthetic.
- **Anti-Paralysis Floating Action (Descongelar):** Continuous pill shape for effortless thumb anchoring.

## Components

Components emphasize clear visual affordances, high tactile feedback, and single-action clarity.

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Teal `#1FAF8A`):** Text `#FFFFFF`, full `rounded-xl` geometry, height `52px`. Gentle tactile scale transition on press (`scale(0.98)`). 
- **Descongelar (Anti-Paralysis `#FF8A5C`):** Pill-shaped action bar with wash background `#FFF0EA`, text `#FF8A5C`, and highlighted outline. Emits a soft, slow ambient glow to invite gentle touch during cognitive blocks.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with subtle border `#E8E4DF`, text `#1A1A1E`.

### Cards & Focus Containers
- Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards resting on `#F6F1FF`. 
- Borders are consistently `1px solid #E8E4DF`.
- In-progress focus containers gain a faint `#E0F7EF` wash border to orient the user back to their active step without jarring animations.

### Chips & Tags
- **Estado (Status):** Height `32px`, corner radius `16px`.
- **Routines / Wash Tags:** Background `#F0EDF9`, label `#8B7BC8`.
- **Completed:** Background `#E0F7EF`, label `#1FAF8A`.

### Inputs & Micro-Checkboxes
- **Checkboxes:** Generous `28px × 28px` hit targets with a 48px tactile perimeter. Rounded `8px` squircle with `2px` border `#E8E4DF`. When checked: fills with `#1FAF8A` alongside a gentle haptic vibration and subtle spring transition.
- **Input Fields:** Minimum height `56px`, `#FFFFFF` surface, placeholder text in `#6B6560`. Focus state replaces standard outline with a clean 2px `#1FAF8A` ring and `#E0F7EF` inner glow.

### Iconography
- Consistent 24px outlined style (Material Symbols Outline standard, stroke weight 1.75px), avoiding complex dual-tone fills. Strokes share the typography hierarchy: `#1A1A1E` for interactive items, `#6B6560` for ambient indications.

### Specialized Component: Micro-Passo (Bite-Sized Task Breakdown)
- A segmented single-item container that displays only the immediate next micro-action (e.g., *"Abrir o documento"* instead of *"Escrever relatório final"*). Features a prominent forward arrow and an optional "Dividir em menores" (Break down further) button.
